This app does not collect or store personal information. We do NOT collect:

2. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob (Advertising)

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob automatically collects the Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier) for ad targeting. This data is collected and shared by AdMob directly — not by us.

To opt out: Android Settings → Google → Ads → Reset Advertising ID

See Google Privacy Policy.

Crash Reporting

Anonymous crash logs are collected automatically to improve app stability. These logs contain no personal information and are not shared with third parties.

3. Data Retention

We do not store any personal data on our servers. Regarding third-party data:

  • Advertising ID (AdMob): Retained by Google according to Google's data retention policies. You can reset your Advertising ID at any time via device settings.
  • Crash logs: Retained for up to 90 days for the purpose of diagnosing and fixing technical issues, after which they are automatically deleted.

4. Data Deletion

Since we do not collect or store personal data, there is no personal account or profile to delete.

To remove data associated with this app:

  • Crash logs: Automatically deleted after 90 days. To request earlier deletion, email erickotieno3@yahoo.com with subject "Delete My Data" and we will process your request within 30 days.
  • Advertising ID: Reset via Android Settings → Google → Ads → Reset Advertising ID. This severs the link between your device and any prior ad targeting data.
  • All local app data: Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data from your device immediately.

This app contains affiliate links. Clicking one may allow the partner site to track the referral. We earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. We do not share personal data via affiliate links.
